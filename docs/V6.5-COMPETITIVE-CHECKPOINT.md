# v6.5 competitive-development checkpoint — 21 October 2026

Status: **candidate, not promoted**. Data reported from CAMBC 3.0.1 local engine, versus immutable `Bot4Frozen` rebuilt from `bots/Bot1/Bot1-v4.zip`; no reserved-map tests were used.

## Historical search and integration

- Core `Bot6` is modular but its planner still shares significant historical infrastructure with v4; do not describe it as a fully new learned team optimizer.
- The full 22-roster search on 17 training maps selected **Prism, Tavi, Vesper Vale** as the best screened policy (`Bot6R09`).
- The roster was searched on six training maps and then compared on eleven other training maps, both sides.
- `Bot6R09` with `V6_MACRO_MODE=1` and `V6_BANK_THRESHOLD=0` beat v4 in **21/34 games**, by **+82.4 average submitted gems**, no engine faults or caught errors. Wilson 95% win-fraction interval **0.450–0.761**. It is not evidence of a clear population-level dominant edge after candidate selection.
- Rebuilt this exact draft and two compile-time flags in `develop/v6.5-prism-tavi-vesper`. A separate 3.0.1 CI build and game run **reproduced 21/34 wins and +82.35 average gem margin** versus v4, with 6/6 versus starter and 1/2 versus frozen v5. The 34 matches use the same 17 maps, not an independent unexposed test pool, so **not an independent generalization replicate**. Run: https://github.com/MohnishDhangar/Cambc/actions/runs/39496568999

## Why no promotion yet

- Win rate is 61.8% versus this specific frozen v4 opponent and map pool. It is promising, not decisive; under a fixed 34-trial binomial model, the 95% interval overlaps 50%. Repeated maps/opponents and search selection make uncertainty greater.
- 399 stdout `fuel spill at` messages were recorded across the 34 games. Although bot faults and caught errors were zero, such warnings must not be suppressed from telemetry.
- A separate experiment using 75k/95k strict fuel reserves (`develop/v6.4-fuel-guards`) eliminated all fuel warnings but crashed strategic performance to **0/34**, mean **−914.65 gems**. This is a **rejected** heuristic intervention, not a change to merge. It demonstrates that zero warnings alone cannot serve as the optimizer's objective.
- The user-selected final holdout is exactly `00lanes`, `01corridors`, `01hooks`, and `01islands`, all sealed.

## Next: interaction search

`develop/v6.6-mode-bank-search` tests twelve (macro-mode × banking-threshold) policies, with a disjoint eight-map selection and nine-map evaluation split **within the 17 training maps**. `tools/search_v6_policy_6_6.py` records all match outcomes, score margins and errors. A successful stage-one score is not a promotion. Then perform independent opponent and repeated-seed tests before opening the four-map sealed evaluation.

Proposed promotion threshold: consistent **at least 70% direct wins versus v4**, positive average gem margin, no bot faults/caught errors, and a robustness check against v5 and another strong opponent across previously unused seeds. Because the winner is selected after many policy tests, rely on final untouched evaluation rather than optimistic training win percentages.
