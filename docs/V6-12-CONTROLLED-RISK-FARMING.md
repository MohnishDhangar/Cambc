# v6.12 — Controlled fallback farming

Hypothesis: On maps where every currently available camp fails the strict survival estimate, all champions return to their spawn and take no further objective actions. Allowing a third pass over **unassigned** camps up to 120% of conservative expected damage, for healthy empty-handed champions before round 1600, may restore productive activity without suicidal all-in. Preserve strict safe selection first. Change toggled with `V6_CONTROLLED_FALLBACK`, pinned v6.8 control `0ddd5338b94db824f9f53261a17f020490b7017f`.

Risks: additional deaths, delayed item upgrades and reduced tributes. Benchmark 102 games across 17 authorized training maps both sides versus frozen v4 and pinned v6.8 before promotion. Four sealed maps remain excluded even if they appeared in uploaded user field archives. Results and negative experiments are recorded without altering v4/v5/v6.8.
