## Methodology and limitations

- “Focus opportunity” counts two *different* allied actors dealing damage to the same champion in the same round; it excludes knock-on splash or shield damage when no distinct actor is recorded.
- Early item additions are inferred from item-array changes in recorded champion turns; they can miss purchases on turns not represented by the extracted timeline.
- Proximity to Bot1 spawn does not prove intentional invasion; it can reflect normal farming or objective placement. Compare with map geometry and several matches before treating it as an invasion policy.
- This report investigates selected extreme cases, not a random sample of opponents. Use the separate 245-game metrics to evaluate prevalence.
